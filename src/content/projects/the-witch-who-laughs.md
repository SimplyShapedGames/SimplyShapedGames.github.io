## You will hear her differently.

The Witch Who Laughs changes the sound of a familiar encounter in Left 4 Dead 2. Her passive crying is replaced by sixteen laughs: eight for the sitting Witch and eight for the wandering Witch.

## A change in atmosphere, not behaviour

The recordings have been remastered with matched loudness and smoother entrances and endings. Host-side playback cycles through a shuffled pool, avoids consecutive repeats and lets a laugh finish before a quiet gap.

The Witch's AI, movement, attacks, pain sounds, music and models are left alone. The focus is the uneasy sound of hearing her nearby.

## Playing with others

The host or server must run the mod for its controlled playback, and listeners need the audio assets. Subscribing as a client does not force the playback script onto an arbitrary remote server.

Find the published version and its installation details on Steam Workshop.
