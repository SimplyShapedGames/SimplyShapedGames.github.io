## A reopening message in one page.

Completed on **9 June 2026**, this portrait flyer brought Gymboree Geneva's reopening information into one clear layout. It combines the free-trial announcement with booking details, the venue address and an overview of the available activities.

## Clear from top to bottom

The design moves from the Gymboree identity and reopening headline to practical information, then closes with activity imagery and the class list. Orange, red and yellow sections separate the content while keeping it aligned with the supplied brand materials.

## Adapting existing material

This work arranged supplied Gymboree branding and photography for the Geneva venue. It does not claim authorship of Gymboree's brand identity or the underlying photographs.

The campaign date and contact information are preserved as part of the completed design and should not be treated as a current offer.
