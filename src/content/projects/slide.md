## Simple yet fun.

Slide is a minimalist puzzle game built around one action: swipe your shape through a maze and find a route to the exit. A simple visual language of colour, walls and movement keeps the focus on the next decision.

## One idea, different challenges

Timed levels, rotating stages, hidden walls, continuous movement and procedurally generated puzzles change how you approach the route. Some challenges ask for careful planning; others add pressure or ask you to read the maze differently.

## Make it your own

Progress unlocks colourful skins, trails, effects and player shapes, including the circle, square, triangle, star and heart. Quick restarts make it easy to try another route.

## The first SimplyShapedGames release

Built with Unity, Slide is available on Android.
