## Find your shape. Find your way.

ShapeDash brings shape switching to a colourful arcade runner. Move between lanes and change form to make it through the obstacles ahead.

## Read the obstacle. Choose the form.

The run brings two decisions together: where to move and which shape to use. Shift into the right lane, match your form to the opening ahead and keep moving through the course.

Colourful 3D artwork carries the same circle-and-square visual language that runs through SimplyShapedGames' other creations.

## Play on Android

Built in Unity and released for Android, it is the third mobile game from SimplyShapedGames.
