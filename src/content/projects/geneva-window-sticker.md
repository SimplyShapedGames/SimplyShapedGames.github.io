## From page to window.

An adaptation of existing Gymboree artwork for the Geneva venue's window sticker. The work includes French typography, layout adjustments, contact presentation and a branded QR code.

The panoramic format brings the venue's activities and contact details together in one continuous design.

## Designed for a physical space

The adaptation balances readable French wording with the long, horizontal format. Contact information, the venue's activity imagery and a branded QR code are arranged so the design works as one piece across the window.

The work refines the layout and typography of supplied artwork; it does not claim authorship of Gymboree's underlying brand or photographs.

## The finished artwork

The image above shows the final orange-contact version. Its public contact details and QR presentation were part of the final layout pass, and the print-ready document was prepared as a separate deliverable for the venue.
