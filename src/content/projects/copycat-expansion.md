## More possibilities for your builds.

Create: Copycat Expansion lets familiar Minecraft materials do more on Create's copycat blocks. Build paths, farmland and decorative details while keeping control of the shape and appearance of your construction.

## Build, then fine-tune

- **Work the terrain.** Use a shovel or hoe on filled copycats to create path and farmland appearances.
- **Choose the state.** Create's Brass Hand cycles details such as snow cover, farmland moisture, lit furnaces, honey levels and bookshelf arrangements.
- **Set the profile.** Switch terrain materials between the copycat's shape and the original material's lowered profile. With Copycats+, connected paths and farmland can continue across stairs and slopes.
- **Keep the building flow.** Ordinary Create wrench behaviour and material refunds remain part of the workflow.

Additional appearances include furnaces, beehives, bookshelves, suspicious blocks and sculk catalysts. These are **decorative material appearances**, not working inventories, cooking systems, bees or loot containers.

## Before you install

The published release targets **Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge**. **Create is required**; **Copycats+ is optional**. Use the CurseForge release page for the current files, version requirements and installation details.
