## A little piece of play.

PiecePerfect is a colourful piece-placement puzzle game. Drag sets of pieces onto a grid, consider how their colours fit together and work out a solution at your own pace.

## A different kind of fit

Rotate pieces before placing them, mix primary colours and work with shifting colour patterns. Limited moves make each placement matter, but there is no timer pushing you to rush.

Your robot companion reacts as you play. When a board needs another approach, a set of tools changes the possibilities: the wand helps with placement, the saw splits pieces, glue merges them and the brush swaps them.
