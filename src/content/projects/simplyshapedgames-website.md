## A place for every creation.

The SimplyShapedGames website brings the company's released games, mods, websites and design work together in one place.

Circles, squares and triangles come from the company logo. Rounded typography, generous space and a choice of light or dark mode carry that identity across the pages.

The site generates static pages for GitHub Pages. Project descriptions and journal posts live in Markdown, so the collection can grow without rebuilding each page by hand.

## Browse the whole collection

Games, mods, websites and design work each have their own category. Project pages bring together descriptions, genuine artwork and galleries, with store or showcase links where they are available.

Search covers the catalogue and the journal, while related projects make it easy to move between connected creations.

## A familiar identity, on every page

The original logo is retained, with rounded lettering and simple geometric details used throughout. A visible theme switch follows you while scrolling, and remembers your preference between the white and nearly black versions.

The original release posts, RSS feed, privacy policy and older article addresses remain part of the site. It is a static GitHub Pages project, with no server or account needed to explore the collection.
