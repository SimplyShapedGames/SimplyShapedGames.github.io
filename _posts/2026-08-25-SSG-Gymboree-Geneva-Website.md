Not every web project starts with a blank page. Sometimes the most useful work is improving the details of a website people already use.

I've been working on **Gymboree Geneva's existing WordPress website**, across its English and French pages. This round of updates improved the hero-image framing, repaired the French birthday-page route and corrected several navigation and language links. Expired seasonal material was also cleared away.

The aim was straightforward: help families find the right information without getting stuck on a broken route or a mismatched page.

This is maintenance and targeted development within Gymboree's existing brand and website—not a claim to have designed the original site. My contribution here is specific to Geneva.

[See the project](/projects/gymboree-geneva/) or visit the [English](https://gymboreeclasses-dach.com/geneva/) and [French](https://gymboreeclasses-dach.com/fr/geneve/) pages.
