**Create: Copycat Expansion** adds more ways to finish a Minecraft build without giving up the flexibility of copycat shapes.

Use a shovel or hoe to turn suitable filled copycats into path and farmland appearances. Create's Brass Hand gives you control over visual states such as snow cover, moisture, furnace lighting and bookshelf arrangements. Terrain materials can also switch between the copycat's shape and the original material's lowered profile.

The first release targets **Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge**. Create is required; Copycats+ is optional and adds support for more shapes and connected terrain profiles.

These are decorative material appearances, not replacement inventories, cooking systems or bee behaviour. The focus is on giving builders more control over the look of a surface.

[Download the initial release on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-copycat-expansion/files/8787849), or [explore the project gallery](/projects/copycat-expansion/).
