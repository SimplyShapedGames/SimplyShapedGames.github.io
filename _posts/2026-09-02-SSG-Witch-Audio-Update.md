The Witch Who Laughs has received a substantial audio refresh since its original Workshop debut.

The current release includes **sixteen laughs**, split between the sitting and wandering Witch. The recordings have been remastered with more consistent loudness and smoother entrances and endings.

Host-side playback now works through a shuffled pool, avoids consecutive repeats and gives each laugh room to finish before a quiet gap. The change is about atmosphere and variation; her AI, movement, attacks, pain sounds, music and models are left alone.

For multiplayer, the host or server needs the playback mod, and listeners need its audio assets. A client subscription cannot impose that playback behaviour on an unrelated server.

The updated release and installation notes are on [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3559560048).
