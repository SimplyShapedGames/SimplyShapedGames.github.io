The Witch is unsettling enough when you hear her crying. What if you heard laughter instead?

**The Witch Who Laughs** is my sound mod for Left 4 Dead 2, now on Steam Workshop. It changes the mood of her passive encounters by replacing the familiar cries with eerie laughter.

This is an audio-focused change. The idea is to make an encounter you already know feel unfamiliar again, without turning it into a different character or redesigning the game around her.

You can find the mod, installation information and release notes on its [Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3559560048).

Thanks for listening—and watch where you step.
