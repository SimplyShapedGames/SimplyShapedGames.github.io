This one is made for a physical space: a long-format **window sticker for Gymboree Geneva**.

Working from supplied Gymboree artwork, I refined the French typography, layout and contact panel. The finished orange-contact version brings the activity imagery, public contact details and branded QR code together across one continuous design.

It is a very different format from a game screen or a web page. The challenge is keeping the information readable while giving the whole strip a consistent rhythm.

The final print-ready adaptation is complete. The underlying photographs and Gymboree identity remain the venue's existing materials; my work is the layout and typography adaptation.
