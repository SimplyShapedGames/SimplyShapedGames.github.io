On **9 June 2026**, I completed a portrait reopening flyer for Gymboree Geneva.

The page uses a tall collage format. The top introduces the Gymboree identity with activity photography, the middle prioritises the reopening announcement, trial-class details and Geneva contact information, and the lower panels carry the venue address and class list.

Orange, red and yellow sections keep the practical details distinct while bringing the supplied brand materials and photographs into one composition.

The dates and contact details are preserved as part of the finished design. The September trial-class message is historical campaign content, not a current offer.

This was layout and adaptation work using supplied Gymboree branding and photography; it does not claim authorship of the underlying identity or images.
